# ICRG Digital Platform

A modern, SEO-first, high-performance business services platform built for ICRG Tax & Legal Services LLP.

The platform is designed to provide business registration, GST, taxation, legal, compliance, accounting, and consulting services through a scalable and user-friendly digital experience.

## Project Vision

The objective of this project is to transform the existing website into a modern lead-generation and business growth platform that supports ICRG's expansion over the next five years.

The platform focuses on:

* Professional service presentation
* Search engine optimization (SEO)
* Lead generation and enquiry management
* Improved user experience
* Mobile-first design
* High performance and accessibility
* Scalable content management
* Future-ready architecture
